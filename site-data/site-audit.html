Module 01
Full Website Understanding & Vision Gap Analysis

Before recommending improvements, we must first diagnose what the website currently says versus what it should say. The gap between these two things is large — and it is the root cause of every other issue on the site.

What the Current Website Communicates

The current website presents SRI GREEN as a premium artisanal food company — somewhere between a small-batch specialty food producer and a sustainability-focused farming cooperative. The copy emphasizes handcrafted quality, local farming heritage, community upliftment, and organic purity. The aesthetic feels craft-food-market rather than industrial B2B export supplier.

Most critically: the footer of every single page reads "SRI GREEN Artisanal Bakery. Handcrafted with joy." — which is catastrophically off-brand for a powder export company. This is a template remnant that was never removed, and it instantly destroys credibility with any serious international buyer.

What the Current Target Audience Seems To Be

Based on current messaging, the implied audience is specialty food enthusiasts, health-conscious retail consumers, and perhaps small food startups looking for artisanal ingredient supplies. The tone, vocabulary, and emotional appeal all point toward B2C retail — the opposite of the intended B2B export buyer.

Module 04
SEO Audit — Technical, On-Page & Content

The SEO situation on this website is the most critical problem outside of branding. Currently, the site is essentially invisible to search engines. Every page has the title "Create Next App" — meaning no page will ever rank for any relevant query.

Technical SEO Issues

🔴
All page titles = "Create Next App"

This is a Next.js default that was never overridden. Every page is effectively unranked for every relevant keyword. This is the single highest-priority SEO fix needed.

🔴
Meta description = "Generated by create next app"

Every page has the same default meta description. Google will either generate its own (often poorly) or ignore the pages. No click-through intent is signaled.

🔴
No structured data (JSON-LD schema)

No Organization schema, no Product schema, no BreadcrumbList. These are essential for a product catalog site to appear properly in search results and Shopping features.

  • Image alt texts use generic AI-generated descriptions rather than keyword-rich product descriptions
  • No sitemap.xml visible — essential for crawlability
  • No robots.txt visible
  • Images served from Google's Aida CDN (lh3.googleusercontent.com) — these are AI-generated placeholder images, not real product photos
  • No canonical tags
  • Domain: agri-expo.vercel.app — a Vercel subdomain has weaker domain authority than a custom domain; migrate to srigreen.com or srigreenexports.com
  • No Open Graph or Twitter Card metadata for social sharing

Module 06
Branding & Positioning Audit

Brand Clarity

The brand identity has a significant internal contradiction. On one hand, the company is called "SRI GREEN Industries" and talks about global export. On the other, the footer says "Artisanal Bakery," the copy uses words like "warmth," "wonder," "artisanal," and "handcrafted" — and the visual system uses ingredient close-up photography that looks more at home on a food blog than an export company website.

The brand needs to make a clear choice: it is an industrial ingredient exporter, not an artisanal food producer. These are not compatible identities.
